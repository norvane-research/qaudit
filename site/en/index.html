The five questions put to your results

Is chance ruled out?

Deflated Sharpe: does the performance survive the number of variants you tried before keeping this one?

And with bad luck?

10,000 block replays of the same trades: in the 5 % least favourable scenarios, do you still win?

Do the losses come clustered?

Your drawdown compared with thousands of reorderings of the same trades.

Would a system with no edge do as well?

Systems with no edge at all, same sizes of gains and losses: how many beat you?

Does it hold outside the periods it was tuned on?

Walk-forward on your parameter sets: what is left of the performance out of sample.

Plus eleven detectors of the way the trading is done: martingale, grid, missing stops, profit concentration, too-smooth curve, asymmetry, single-period dependence, unrealistic holding times and, with quotes, look-ahead, impossible prices, over-sensitive stop.

The rules

Frequently asked questions

Why pay if the engine is free?

Running the engine yourself gives you the verdict. An attestation is worth something else: a third party re-ran the computation on your file and recorded it in a public registry that your buyers check without taking your word.

Does my source code stay confidential?

We do not need it. The audit covers your trades, not your code: you never hand over your strategy.

Why insist so much on the number of variants tested?

Because it is the question nobody asks and it eliminates most systems. A result obtained after three hundred attempts is not worth the same result obtained first time. The report shows from how many attempts your result stops being significant.

And if my system is good?

You receive the numbered attestation, recorded in the registry, and the drawdown benchmarks: the loss level beyond which your system no longer behaves as in its history. Often the most useful part of the report, even for a sound system.

What happens to my data?

The source file is deleted 30 days after delivery; the archive kept contains no name, no account number, no individual trade. Data policy.
